Unmatched Hydrophobic Water Beading & Self-Cleaning

One of the most noticeable benefits of a professional ceramic coating is its extraordinary hydrophobic (water-repelling) performance. Uncoated clear coats possess an irregular microscopic texture with millions of peaks and valleys where water, road grime, and dust become trapped. This creates low contact angles, causing dirty water to puddle and dry into unsightly mineral water spots on your finish.

Our professional ceramic coatings fill these microscopic surface irregularities, creating an ultra-smooth, high-density surface layer. Water droplets cannot spread across the surface; instead, they bead up into tight, spherical beads with contact angles exceeding 110 degrees, rolling off body panels under the slightest airflow or gravitational pull.

As water beads roll across coated panels, they capture loose surface dust, dirt, and road grime, washing them away naturally in what is known as the self-cleaning effect. During a rainstorm, your vehicle actively sheds dirt, staying noticeably cleaner between scheduled washes.

Routine maintenance washes become effortless and fast. Mud, road dust, and bug remnants cannot bond tightly to the slick ceramic surface, allowing you to wash and dry your vehicle in half the time without aggressive scrubbing or harsh chemical cleaners.

Multi-Stage Paint Correction for Flawless Gloss

Applying a ceramic coating over swirled, scratched, or oxidized paint locks those imperfections beneath the coating indefinitely. Because ceramic coatings form a semi-permanent crystalline bond, surface preparation is the single most critical phase of the entire application process.

Our certified detailers execute comprehensive multi-stage paint correction before applying any ceramic coating. We begin with a thorough chemical and mechanical decontamination, utilizing foam cannons, pH-neutral citrus washes, fallout-dissolving iron removers, and fine clay bars to extract embedded rail dust, industrial fallout, and road tar.

Once decontaminated, our technicians measure paint clear coat depth using digital ultrasonic gauges to ensure safe polishing parameters. We then deploy dual-action machine polishers and specialized compound and pad combinations to level micro-imperfections, eliminating up to 90% or more of swirl marks, light scratches, oxidation, and buffer trails.

The final jewel-polishing stage refines the clear coat to a brilliant, mirror-smooth gloss. Only when the paint finish has achieved true optical depth and clarity do we proceed to apply the ceramic coating, sealing in absolute perfection.

Resistance to UV Oxidation & Chemical Etching

Southeastern Michigan's climate subjects vehicle surfaces to extreme environmental stressors. In the summer, relentless ultraviolet (UV) radiation breaks down organic paint pigments and clear coat binders, causing vibrant colors to fade and clear coats to chalk or oxidize, particularly on hoods and roofs.

Warm weather also brings chemical threats like acidic bird droppings, caustic tree sap, and baked-on bug splatter. When these acidic substances heat up under direct sun, they chemically burn into the clear coat within hours, creating permanent etched scars that cannot be washed away.

Professional nano-ceramic coatings provide durable chemical and UV defense. The dense crystalline SiO2 structure acts as a sacrificial barrier with high thermal resistance and broad chemical stability, resisting pH levels ranging from pH 2 to pH 12.

Ultraviolet radiation is deflected before it can penetrate and oxidize vehicle paint, preventing premature color fading and clear coat breakdown. Caustic environmental acids and road brines sit safely atop the ceramic shield, giving you ample time to wipe them away without fear of permanent clear coat damage.

Deep Mirror Gloss & Candy-Like Visual Enhancement

Beyond physical and chemical protection, ceramic coatings provide an unmistakable aesthetic transformation that elevates any vehicle's presence. Traditional waxes sit on top of the clear coat as a temporary oily film, offering fleeting shine that diminishes after a few rainstorms or wash cycles.

Our professional ceramic coatings form an optically clear, glass-like layer that bonds molecularly to the clear coat. By creating a perfectly flat, uniform surface, the coating maximizes light reflection and eliminates optical dispersion, giving the paint an incredible liquid-wet candy appearance.

Metallic and pearlescent paint colors pop with astonishing visual depth and clarity. Flakes within metallic finishes sparkle vividly under sunlight, while non-metallic dark paints—such as deep blacks, blues, and reds—gain deep, rich reflection reminiscent of a fresh pool of liquid paint.

This dazzling mirror finish does not wash off or degrade after a few weeks. The glass-like luster remains locked in, allowing your car to turn heads at local car shows or daily commutes around Waterford for years to come.

Prestige Protective Films offers dedicated ceramic coatings tailored for specific vehicle surfaces. Our high-temperature wheel coatings are formulated to withstand extreme heat generated by high-performance braking systems. Coated wheels repel hot brake dust, iron shavings, and road grime, preventing brake dust etching and making wheel cleaning as simple as a pressure rinse.

Our hydrophobic glass coatings bond to front windshields, side glass, and rear windows, repelling rainwater at highway speeds and dramatically improving wet-weather visibility without wiper chatter. Raindrops bead up and fly off the windshield, providing clearer sightlines and increased safety during heavy Michigan downpours.

Unpainted exterior plastic and rubber trim pieces are especially vulnerable to UV fading, turning chalky and grey over time. Our trim ceramic coatings restore deep, factory-black color while shielding plastic porous structures against UV oxidation and weather cracking.

Waterford's Certified Professional Coating Studio

1. How long does a professional ceramic coating last?
Depending on the specific package chosen and your maintenance routine, professional ceramic coatings last anywhere from 2 to 7+ years. Unlike temporary waxes that wash off in weeks, ceramic coatings form a permanent chemical bond with your clear coat that does not wash off.
2. Does ceramic coating prevent rock chips and scratches?
No, ceramic coatings are engineered to protect against chemical stains, UV oxidation, bird droppings, water spots, and micro-marring. For high-impact physical protection against rock chips, gravel strikes, and heavy scratches, we recommend installing Paint Protection Film (PPF) beneath the ceramic coating.
3. Why is paint correction necessary before ceramic coating?
Ceramic coatings are semi-permanent and optically clear. Applying a coating over swirl marks, scratches, or oxidation permanently seals those flaws beneath the ceramic glass layer. Multi-stage paint correction removes these defects, ensuring the paint is flawless before sealing.
4. Can a ceramic coating be applied to wheels and exterior glass?
Yes. We offer specialized ceramic formulations engineered for high-temperature wheel surfaces to repel hot brake dust, as well as ultra-slick glass ceramic coatings that improve wet-weather driving visibility by repelling rain.
5. How should I wash my car after it has been ceramic coated?
Washing a ceramic-coated vehicle is effortless. Use a pH-neutral automotive shampoo, a soft microfiber wash mitt, and the two-bucket wash method. Avoid abrasive automatic brush car washes, which can induce micro-scratches on any surface.
6. Does ceramic coating improve vehicle resale value?
Yes, professional ceramic coating preserves factory paint in pristine showroom condition and prevents seasonal degradation. Our ceramic coating installations are registered with warranty documentation and can be recorded on CARFAX reports.
